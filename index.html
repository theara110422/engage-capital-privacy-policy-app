Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 21, 2026

Engage Capital Cambodia Co., Ltd. ("Engage Capital", "we", "us", "our") operates the Engage Capital mobile app and website (the "Service"), providing digital microfinance lending to customers in Cambodia. This Privacy Policy explains what personal data we collect, why we collect it, how it is used and protected, and the choices you have — including how to delete your account.

1. Information We Collect

To open a credit line and process loan applications, we collect:

  • Identity documents — National ID (front/back), a selfie for identity verification, and guarantor identification documents where applicable.
  • Financial information — declared and verified monthly income, bank statements, pay slips, employment letters, and bank account details (ABA, Wing) used for disbursement and repayment.
  • Contact and profile information — full name, phone number, email address, date of birth, gender, and emergency/security contact details.
  • Loan and transaction data — credit line usage, withdrawal requests, repayment history, and account balances.
  • Device and usage data — device identifiers, app version, IP address, crash logs, and push-notification tokens, collected to secure the Service and improve reliability.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To verify your identity and assess creditworthiness before approving a loan.
  • To process withdrawals, disbursements, and repayments.
  • To communicate with you about your account, loan status, and repayment reminders.
  • To detect and prevent fraud, and to meet our legal and regulatory obligations.
  • To improve the Service and provide customer support.

3. Sharing Your Information

We do not sell your personal data. We share information only:

  • With regulators, including the Securities and Exchange Commission of Cambodia (SECC) and the National Bank of Cambodia (NBC), as legally required.
  • With partner banks (ABA, Wing) solely to process disbursements and repayments.
  • With service providers who process data on our behalf (e.g. cloud hosting, SMS/OTP delivery), under confidentiality obligations.
  • When required by law, court order, or to protect the rights, property, or safety of Engage Capital, our customers, or others.

4. Data Retention

We retain loan, repayment, and transaction records for as long as required by Cambodian financial recordkeeping and anti-money-laundering regulations, even after you delete your account. Personal identification data not needed for these legal purposes is deleted or anonymized when your account is deleted.

5. Your Rights & Account Deletion

You can review and update your profile information at any time from within the app. You may request deletion of your account and personal data in either of the following ways:

  • In-app: open the Engage Capital app → Profile → Danger Zone → Delete Account, and confirm.
  • By email: send a request from your registered email/phone to engagecapitalcambodia@gmail.com.

Note: accounts with an outstanding loan balance or a pending withdrawal request cannot be deleted until the balance is settled or the request is resolved. Once deleted, your login credentials, contact details, and profile data are removed or anonymized. Loan and repayment records tied to your account are retained as required by law, but are no longer linked to an active, accessible account.

6. Data Security

We use industry-standard safeguards — including encrypted network transport, access controls, and secure credential storage — to protect your data from unauthorized access, alteration, or disclosure. No method of transmission or storage is 100% secure, and we continuously work to strengthen our protections.

7. Children's Privacy

The Service is intended for users who are at least 18 years old. We do not knowingly collect personal data from minors.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Material changes will be reflected by updating the "Last updated" date above, and where appropriate, communicated in-app.

9. Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or how your data is handled, contact us at engagecapitalcambodia@gmail.com.
